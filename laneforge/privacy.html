Lane Forge — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-20 · App ID: com.willio.jeanvilma.laneforge

Summary

Lane Forge is an offline-first, 100% free fortress strategy game. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, and no account sign-in. Progress is stored on your device. The only optional network use is crash reporting when Firebase Crashlytics is configured for a release build.

Data stored on your device

  • Game progress (city, resources, troops, heroes, campaign stars, goals)
  • Audio settings (mute, music / SFX volumes)
  • Language preference

This data stays on your device unless you clear app storage or use Settings → Reset progress.

What we do not do

  • Show ads or use advertising IDs for ads
  • Sell personal information
  • Require email, phone, or social login
  • Run payments or a gem shop inside the app

Crash reporting (optional)

When Firebase is configured for a non-debug release build, Lane Forge may send crash and diagnostic breadcrumbs to Firebase Crashlytics (device/OS metadata and crash stacks). If Firebase is not configured, the game runs fully offline and reporting is skipped.

Children

Lane Forge is aimed at a general audience (cartoon siege combat). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for marketing.

Your choices

  • Clear app data or uninstall to remove local saves
  • Use Reset progress in Settings to wipe game progress
